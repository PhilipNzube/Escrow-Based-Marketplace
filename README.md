# Escrow-Based Marketplace

## About the Project

The Escrow-Based Marketplace is a web application project built around the concept of making online transactions safer through an escrow-style process.

In a typical online transaction, a buyer may be concerned about paying for something before receiving it, while a seller may be concerned about handing over a product or service without receiving payment. An escrow arrangement is intended to reduce this uncertainty by introducing agreed conditions around when funds are released.

This project explores how that concept can be represented in a marketplace experience through a web interface.

## The Problem It Addresses

Trust is an important part of online transactions. When buyers and sellers do not know each other, both parties may be concerned about the risk of the other person failing to fulfil their side of the agreement.

An escrow-based model aims to provide a more structured transaction process by defining what needs to happen before a transaction is considered complete.

The marketplace concept focuses on this challenge and explores how a digital platform could support that type of interaction.

## Project Goals

- Explore a marketplace experience built around escrow-based transactions.
- Present the idea of a more structured buying and selling process.
- Provide a web interface for demonstrating the marketplace concept.
- Establish a foundation for further development of transaction-related workflows.
- Explore how a web application can represent trust-focused features in an online marketplace.

## How the Concept Works

An escrow-based transaction generally follows a process such as:

1. **Agreement:** The buyer and seller agree on the product or service and the conditions of the transaction.
2. **Payment:** The buyer makes payment through the designated payment process.
3. **Fulfilment:** The seller provides the product or service according to the agreement.
4. **Confirmation:** The transaction reaches the agreed completion stage.
5. **Release:** Funds are released according to the escrow arrangement.

This describes the general escrow model. The actual handling of funds, transaction verification, dispute resolution, and release conditions depend on the backend and payment integrations implemented in the application.

## Potential Use Cases

A marketplace built around an escrow model could be adapted for transactions involving goods, freelance services, digital products, or other exchanges where buyers and sellers need clearly defined transaction conditions.

The suitability of each use case would depend on the final implementation and its operational requirements.

## Project Scope

The project focuses on the web application side of an escrow-based marketplace concept. It provides a codebase and live demonstration that can be reviewed as a starting point for further development.

A production version would need appropriate server-side transaction management, secure payment-provider integration, identity and access controls, and carefully defined dispute-handling procedures before it could be relied upon to hold or release real funds.
